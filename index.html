The Local Artist & Pop-Up Radar

Connecting Clients & Local Artists

Welcome to HennaLink—solving the gap between clients looking for immediate local body art and artists promoting live pop-ups, markets, and walk-in slots without relying on messy social media algorithms.

For Clients

Fast map-based discovery to find local artists working nearby this weekend, check live availability, and grab quick walk-in slots.

For Artists

High-visibility local broadcasting tool to share temporary pop-up locations, flash sheets, and capture walk-in traffic directly.

Core Features (MVP)

📍 The Live Radar Map: Geolocation map with color-coded pins (🔴 Active Pop-Up right now, 🔵 Weekend Market, 🟣 Salon Walk-Ins).
⚡ "Open Now" Broadcasts: Quick toggles allowing artists to drop a temporary 4-hour pin showing exactly where they are set up.
🌿 Artist Profiles & Flash Catalogs: Clean display of portfolios, natural ingredients vs. jagua, pricing, and direct booking links.
